Simple by design

Extra light, when your display has room.

A small control in your menu bar gives you a boost slider, live capability status, and an off switch that immediately restores the usual appearance.

☀

Uses real EDR headroom

Reads the built-in display’s potential and current Extended Dynamic Range limits, then keeps the effective gain within what is available.

↗

Stays out of your way

A click-through overlay brightens the desktop while mouse and keyboard input continue to reach your apps. No Dock icon is added.

◎

You stay in control

Boost starts off at launch. Save a preferred slider level, optionally launch at login, and switch off or quit to return to normal.

Before you install

What to expect.

The app works with the display you already have. Results depend on the EDR headroom macOS reports at that moment.

  • Built-in display onlyDesigned for EDR-capable MacBook Pro displays. When headroom is unavailable, the menu explains it and disables boost.
  • Download the build for your MacGitHub Actions produces separate Apple Silicon and Intel app bundles. The builds are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized. See download instructions.
  • Public APIs, open sourceNo screen recording, private display controls, or gamma-table changes. Read the source and MIT license.
